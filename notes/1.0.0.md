- **Review the change you picked.** Pick a pull or merge request anywhere on the canvas and Review shows its diff, the whole change or one commit at a time, with every line a place a comment can go.
- **Write the review, then send it once.** Your comments collect in a panel beside the diff, and you send them all as one review with a verdict, after seeing exactly what will be posted.
- **Agents can draft, you decide.** An agent can add comments to your draft; only you send it.
- **Comments stay where you put them.** Each comment is tied to the exact line and commit it was written on, and Review checks for new pushes just before sending.
- **No extra login.** It reads and posts through the `gh` or `glab` you are already signed in to, so the review is under your own name.
