- **Search the review with ⌘F.** Press ⌘F in Review to open Kehikot's find bar. Every match in the points and their quoted lines is highlighted and counted ("3 of 17"), and the page scrolls to the current one. Enter and ⇧Enter move between matches, and Escape closes the bar. Words you are still typing in a box are not searched.
- **Opens where you left it.** Each Review container remembers how far down the review you had scrolled, and comes back there when the kehikko is opened again.
- **No flash of "Asking…".** Kehikot keeps its cover over the container until the change and its review are read and drawn, then shows them whole.
- **A page that fails says so.** If Review ever fails to draw, Kehikot is told and shows what went wrong instead of an empty container.
